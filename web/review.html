    What the picture means
    The point cloud uses measured neuron-body positions. Bright points are a sample of neurons that fired in the displayed 25 ms window. Camera movement is not brain activity.
    Lines are sampled, real connections drawn straight between cell bodies. They are schematic, not reconstructed axon shapes. Signals entering the scene are a diagram, not a recording of individual synapses.
    The simulation uses all 176,422 neurons and 6,287,749 retained connections. The visualization is downsampled. Each trial starts from rest; there is no learning.
    Data: male CNS v1.0, by HHMI Janelia, Cambridge / MRC LMB, and Google Research, CC BY 4.0. Connections below five synapses and autapses were removed by the upstream derivative. Plot coordinates are rounded to the nearest voxel.
    Real connectivity does not establish understanding or useful review quality. Disabling synapses tests whether the network participated, not whether it is intelligent.

    A changed result only establishes a regression when the original satisfies the explicit contract. No counterexample in the sampled cases is not proof of correctness. These bundled examples are not a general repository integration.
